About me

Background

I'm a Computer Game Design student at George Mason University, working toward my BFA with an expected graduation in May 2028. Before Mason, I earned an associate degree in architecture at Northern Virginia Community College, where I also competed in the college esports program. Since moving into game design, I've worked across gameplay programming in Unity, 3D character modeling and animation, and audio, from sound design to voice acting. I've built complete projects both solo and with a team, and I'm looking for opportunities to keep growing in all three areas.

Skill set

Game development: Unity, gameplay systems, scene and UI integration, HTML/CSS/JavaScript

3D art and animation: 3ds Max, Biped rigging and keyframe animation, Substance Painter, Photoshop texturing, FBX export to Unity

Audio: sound design, music and sound effect recreation, voice acting, Adobe Premiere
