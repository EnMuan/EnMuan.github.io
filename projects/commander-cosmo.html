Commander Cosmo — 3D Model

3D Art

About this project

A game-ready character model built in 3ds Max at roughly 1,750 polygons, textured in Photoshop with a hand-painted diffuse map. Rigged with Biped and animated with keyframes, including walk, run, idle, jump, and death cycles.
