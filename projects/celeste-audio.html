Celeste — Audio Remake & VA

Audio

About this project

An audio remake of a scene from Celeste, with the original sound assets replaced to match the source as closely as possible. I recreated the sound effects and music, and performed the character vocalizations myself, voicing both the main character and the old lady. Everything was compiled and synced in Adobe Premiere.
