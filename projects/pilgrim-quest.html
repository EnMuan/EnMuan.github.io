About this project

Pilgrims' Quest is a 2D top-down adventure game based on the original Zelda games, made by a team of five. Players explore three open levels filled with enemies and optional lore, armed with a sword and a wand. I created and sourced the game's sound effects, including ambience, enemy sounds, footsteps, hit sounds, spell effects, and UI sounds, and I made 2D props.

Team: Rainbows (tiles and props art), Danish (character art), Mason Hale (narrative and level design), Reditect (programming), and En Muan (sound effects and 2D props).
